# User guide

Finalyzer is a workspace for tabular data (CSV or Excel): a dashboard, plain-language
insights, and a question box. Every number comes from the data.

## First visit

1. Open the site. If there are no users yet, **Create the first administrator**.
2. Workspace name, your name, e-mail, password of at least 12 characters.
3. Language **UZ | EN** and theme (light / dark / auto) are in the top bar.

## Demo or your files

- **Sample datasets**: retail, bank, sales, statistics. Loading a demo opens the dashboard.
- **Upload**: CSV or XLSX. The summary lists tables, removed personal columns, and the
  detected domain (you can change it). Then **Build dashboard**.

Viewers cannot upload.

## Dashboard

The period is shown at the top. Click a chart slice to add a filter chip; click again to
remove it. Every chart has **View as table**.

## Insights and Ask

Insights: Simple / Expert / Explain simpler.
Ask: click a suggested question. **Verified** means the result was checked against the data.

## Privacy

The Privacy page shows bytes sent outside. In offline mode that number is 0.

Read `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` before relying on the numbers for a decision.
